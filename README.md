# earth-sample-cube

The smallest [EarthRT](https://github.com/threert/EarthRT) place: a 6 m vermilion cube by the Kamo river in Kyoto.
`earthrt.json` says where it is and what it holds (SPEC § 5); `models/cube.glb` is the model.

Registered with `bun tools/register.ts threert/earth-sample-cube` (EarthRT repository), and found by the hourly crawl
through the topic `earthrt-place`. Content: CC0-1.0.
